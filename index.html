This is an interactive fiction that interrogates the unstable territory of implied connection between individuals, moving through states of yearning, avoidance, tenderness, and aversion that emerge in relationships that are felt but not fully secured. Framed as a browser-based letter, the work uses familiar interface elements as structural expressions of refusal and delay. It treats digital systems (typically built around connection and routing) as sites where connection breaks down, drifts, or becomes uncertain, positioning the browser as a contemporary emotional landscape where intimacy is continuously negotiated through partial interaction. Across these constraints, the work follows small, ordinary gestures—minor encounters, unspoken observations, deferred communication. Language is shaped and altered as it passes through infrastructural systems that compress and redirect meaning.

The work considers what persists in the gap between feeling, expression, and the conditions that determine what can actually be conveyed.
